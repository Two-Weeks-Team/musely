About these models

Fan-made 3D recreations of Meta's Muse character Jolly, made for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon demo. Muse and Jolly belong to Meta; these models are not covered by the repository license and are not offered for reuse. Side and back details are interpreted. No live agent is connected.

Nine variants share one body, rig and fleece maps; each adds its own palette, garments, props and a signature action (★). The viewer uses the same runtime files as the world.
