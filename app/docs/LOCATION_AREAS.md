# Owner neighborhood areas

The owner chooses an **area**, while the Muse has a **current place**. A coarse 0.01° cell remains a compatibility/discovery grid, not the owner's movement boundary. This implements the 2026-11-03 area requirement following direction v2 chapters 11 and 13.

- Eight presets define staged 1,500 m activity areas with explicit catalog membership. HQ includes the authored commons and nearby catalog POIs inside its radius. Each of the seven remote Bay areas has its existing entry/activity place plus a reading corner and garden, both explicitly **virtual**. These are map-only authored activities, not new real-POI claims, municipal borders or new 3D scenes.
- An unconnected owner Muse chooses eligible places **inside its area before selection** and walks among them. Reaching an owner grid cell does not stop it. Inside any catalog neighborhood, trips always walk; a pass/token does not upgrade that trip.
- Outside a neighborhood, the existing walking, Transit Pass bus, verified destination area and consumed Taxi Token rules remain. A location change does not move the Muse's current place before arrival. Discovery and trade distance still use the current place.
- A connected owner's idle local stroll is a **rule** action, not an MCP command or a team-model decision. It waits for existing trips/beats, the arrival tick, recent client activity, intents (including an incoming approach), pending Tasks, open campfire invitations and delegation pauses. Other connected Muses keep their previous no-scenario-decision rule; the team-model guard remains in place. Real connected calls retain their command and actor provenance.
- Old cell verification is **not** promoted to area verification. An explicit area stamp and staged owner verification are required. A prior GPS state's catalog target can identify an area without reading device coordinates; it confers no new verification.
- Browser device location is optional and maps locally to the nearest preset area within the existing 15 km proximity limit. Only the area id remains in browser state; outside Bay or denied permission keeps virtual guidance. The page sends no coordinates. The owner API rejects raw coordinate/unknown fields and accepts only a preset id, including for a locally mapped GPS source. GPS verification remains unavailable.
- Owner area and follow anchor remain private in public replay snapshots. `engine-3` and the catalog hash bind the new rules, area bounds and places. The checkpoint-only player also accepts the exact known predecessor tuple (`engine-2`, `harness-c0c69e98b5e8`, `catalog-d69858ec160f`, `run-log-2`), preserving its original versions/hashes without executing the old engine. Unknown or mixed tuples remain incompatible.
- The location chip, Location panel and map distinguish the owner area from the Muse's place. The map area overlay is hidden during recorded replay and story mode. Catalog centers are not device positions.

Tests: `scripts/neighborhood.test.mjs` plus the location, engine, items, MCP, message, run-log and replay regression suites. Production and actual MacBook acceptance are separate from local tests; existing 494 evidence is not relabelled as evidence for this engine.
