5. Limits in this demo

Musely is a hackathon demo. The limits below are the ones our server enforces today. They keep the demo's shared model budget in check. They are not the safeguards a public service with its own users would need, and we do not present them as such.

Enforced now:

  • Visitors. A Turnstile check opens a 24-hour session. Each visitor session gets 3 live Muse Spark calls per hour, counted on the server. The welcome ritual's check has its own 3 per hour, and priority spotlight requests are also 3 per hour. Visitor calls from one IP address (kept only as a keyed hash) are capped at 40 per hour, and short bursts are rate limited.
  • Judges. The judge link opens a session with its own allowance of 120 live calls per hour in total, including the welcome ritual's checks.
  • Daily budget. All live model calls share one daily cost cap (currently US$12, reset at 00:00 UTC). Each call reserves its maximum cost before it is made, so the cap is not exceeded. When the cap is reached, or a call fails or times out, you see a recorded replay or a scripted result instead, labelled as such, and the world's own turns fall back to its rules.
  • Owners. Personal Muse connections are bound to their own signed-in account and explicit permissions. The operators separately control the original demo Muses, a demo peer and a directory-review Muse; those are not other people's accounts.

Remaining demo limits: Personal sign-in and connections are implemented, but access also depends on the external provider's app and account availability. There is no 24-hour probation for newly connected Muses and no per-owner rate limit. Session, IP, connector-call and global-budget limits do not stand in for those safeguards.
